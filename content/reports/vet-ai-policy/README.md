## What this is

A policy research report comparing how AI in vocational education and training (VET) is regulated internationally — UK, Ireland, Finland, Canada, New Zealand, Singapore, China, Japan, the EU, Indonesia, and the US — against Australia's approach (ASQA for VET, TEQSA for higher education).

Covers:
- International best-practice regulatory approaches by country
- The EU AI Act and prohibited practices, implications for ed-tech/social platforms
- US state-level CTE/AI policy (California, New York, Texas, etc.)
- Australia's ASQA vs TEQSA gap analysis
- Recommendations for a coordinated national AI-in-VET approach (joint taskforce, unified guidelines, assessment sandbox trials, etc.)
- Full Harvard reference list
