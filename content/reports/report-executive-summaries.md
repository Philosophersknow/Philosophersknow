# AIIPD Civic Intelligence™ — Report Executive Summary Templates

**Purpose:** Ready-to-adapt executive summary language for each of the 12 AIIPD intelligence report types. Each template is 150–200 words, written in intelligence framing, and designed to be personalised with organisation name, specific findings, and contextual details.

**Usage:** Replace bracketed placeholders with organisation-specific intelligence. Retain the structure and framing language. Adapt the intelligence findings to reflect the specific intelligence picture for the organisation being profiled.
