# CHC33021 — Certificate III in Individual Support
## Qualification Overview and Student Resource Guide

**Training Package:** CHC — Community Services Training Package
**Qualification Code:** CHC33021
**AQFL Level:** Certificate III
**Total Units:** 15 (9 core + 6 elective)
**Typical Duration:** 12–18 months (part-time); 6–9 months (full-time)
**Delivery Mode:** Blended (classroom + workplace placement; minimum 120 hours work placement)

---

## What This Qualification Is

The Certificate III in Individual Support qualifies you to work as a support worker in aged care, disability, or home and community care settings. You will work under supervision to provide person-centred support to people who need assistance with daily living — while respecting their dignity, choices, and independence.

This qualification replaced the older CHC30212 / CHC33015 certificates and reflects current best practice in person-centred care, informed consent, and inclusive support.

**Typical job titles:**
- Personal care worker / care assistant
- Disability support worker
- Community care worker / home care worker
- Residential aged care worker
- Support worker (NDIS)

---

## Entry Requirements

There are no formal entry requirements, though your RTO may require:
- A current Working With Vulnerable People (WWVP) or Working With Children (WWC) check
- National Police Check
- NDIS Worker Screening Check (for disability specialisation)
- Basic literacy and numeracy (usually assessed by the RTO at enrolment)
- First aid certificate (or willingness to obtain during study)

## How You Will Be Assessed

Assessment in CHC33021 uses a combination of methods:

| Assessment Method | What It Tests |
|---|---|
| Written questions (knowledge evidence) | Your understanding of legislation, policies, principles, and theory |
| Case studies | Application of principles to realistic support scenarios |
| Observation / workplace demonstration | That you can actually perform the skills in a real or simulated environment |
| Reflective journal / log | That you reflect on your practice and identify learning |
| Work placement portfolio | 120 hours of supervised practice with documented evidence |
| Third-party reports | Your supervisor confirms your competency in the workplace |

**You must demonstrate competency in ALL elements of ALL units.** There is no averaging. A gap in one element means the unit is Not Yet Competent (NYC).

---

## What Assessors Look For — The Core Principle

The Community Services training package is built on **person-centred practice**. This means the person receiving support — their choices, their dignity, their identity, their goals — is always at the centre of every decision and action.

Every assessment task in this qualification asks the same underlying question: *How do you ensure the person's rights, choices, and wellbeing are protected and promoted in this situation?*

If your answer to any question focuses primarily on what you (the worker) do, without reference to what the person receiving support wants, needs, or has the right to choose — it is incomplete.

---

## Work Placement Requirements

- Minimum **120 hours** of supervised work placement
- Must include direct, hands-on care with real clients in an approved facility or community setting
- Simulated environments can supplement but not replace the 120 hours of real workplace contact
- Your RTO will provide a placement agreement; your supervisor will complete third-party reports
- You must have current first aid and infection control before placement
