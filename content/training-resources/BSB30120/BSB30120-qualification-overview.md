**Training Package:** BSB — Business Services Training Package

**AQF Level:** Certificate III
**Total Units:** 13 (6 core + 7 elective)
**Typical Duration:** 6–12 months (varies significantly by delivery mode)
**Delivery Mode:** Blended / workplace-based / online / classroom

---

## What This Qualification Is

The Certificate III in Business provides the foundational skills and knowledge for working in a wide range of business and administrative roles. It is one of the broadest and most transferable qualifications in the Australian VET system — applicable to virtually every industry sector.

This qualification develops competency in communication, workplace teamwork, digital skills, sustainability, WHS, and critical thinking. It is the entry point for office and administrative careers and a prerequisite or co-requisite for many higher Business qualifications.

**Typical job titles:**
- Office administrator / office assistant
- Administration officer
- Receptionist / customer service officer
- Data entry operator
- Business support officer
- Team support administrator
- Records management officer

---

## Entry Requirements

There are no formal entry requirements for BSB30120. Your RTO may assess literacy and numeracy skills at enrolment. The qualification is suitable for school-leavers, career changers, people returning to work, and workers seeking to formalise existing skills.

## What Makes Business Assessments Different

Business Services assessments are built around **authentic workplace scenarios**. The assessor is checking not just whether you know something but whether you can do something that would be useful in a real workplace.

This means:
- Generic answers do not score well. Your answer should demonstrate *how* you would apply the principle in a specific context.
- Documentation tasks require you to produce actual documents — not just describe what a document should include.
- Communication tasks require evidence that you can communicate effectively, professionally, and appropriately for the audience and purpose.

The key question your assessor is asking: **"Would this person be useful and effective in a real business environment?"**

## Core Themes Across All Units

**1. Professional communication**
Almost every BSB unit involves communication. Professional written and verbal communication is a core expectation throughout the qualification. Poor spelling, grammar, and presentation in written assessment tasks will be noted — not just as a literacy issue, but as evidence of whether you meet workplace standards.

**2. Legislative compliance**
Workplace legislation runs through the entire BSB training package. You are expected to know and reference the Fair Work Act, WHS legislation, Privacy Act, and relevant anti-discrimination legislation across multiple units.

**3. Sustainability and inclusion**
BSB30120 introduced sustainability (BSBSUS211) and inclusion (BSBTWK301) as core units — reflecting employer expectations that all workers contribute to ethical, sustainable, and inclusive workplace practices.

**4. Digital literacy**
Business work is digital work. Proficiency with business software (word processing, spreadsheets, email, presentation tools) is expected throughout the qualification.
