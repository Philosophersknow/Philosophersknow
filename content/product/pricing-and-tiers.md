# AIIPD Civic Intelligence™ — Product Tiers and Pricing Descriptions

**Purpose:** Product tier descriptions for website, proposals, and sales materials. All in intelligence framing. Four-tier structure appropriate for government/consulting SaaS.

---

## TIER STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

AIIPD offers four intelligence access tiers, designed to serve the full range of organisations and professionals who rely on civic intelligence. Each tier provides increasing depth of intelligence access, coverage, and service support.

| Tier | Name | Primary Audience |
|------|------|-----------------|
| 1 | Place Intelligence Essentials | Small-to-medium local government, community organisations |
| 2 | Civic Intelligence Professional | Local government, consulting professionals, regional bodies |
| 3 | Civic Intelligence Enterprise | State government, large local government, consulting firms |
| 4 | Intelligence Partnership | State agencies, peak bodies, major research institutions |

---

## TIER 1: PLACE INTELLIGENCE ESSENTIALS

### Tier Name
Place Intelligence Essentials

### Who It's For
Small-to-medium local government organisations, community organisations, and individual practitioners who need access to foundational civic intelligence for their own organisation and immediate comparator context.

Ideal for: councils with population under 30,000 / community organisations with a defined geographic focus / individual consultants beginning their AIIPD engagement.

### What Intelligence Access Is Included

**Civic Intelligence Centre**
- Intelligence Summary for your primary organisation
- Access to intelligence across all six core dimensions (Governance, Financial, Community, Infrastructure, Environmental, Strategic)
- Intelligence Signals for your primary organisation

**Intelligence Briefs**
- Annual Council Intelligence Profile (1 per year)
- Annual Executive Intelligence Summary (4 per year — quarterly)
- Community Intelligence Profile (1 per year)
- Emerging Risk Intelligence Brief (4 per year — quarterly)
- Access to generate additional brief types (up to 8 briefs per year total)

**Comparative Place Intelligence**
- Access to a defined comparator group (up to 10 organisations selected from the Place Intelligence Database)
- Comparative intelligence across all dimensions for the selected group

**Place Intelligence Database**
- Read access to intelligence for your primary organisation (deep access)
- Read access to overview intelligence for your selected comparator group (summary access)

**Team Access**
- Up to 5 named users
- Intelligence Viewer access for all team members; Intelligence User access for up to 2 members

**Support**
- Access to Intelligence Support resources (guides, video tutorials)
- Email support from AIIPD Intelligence Team
- Annual 60-minute Intelligence Review with an AIIPD Intelligence Partner

### Positioning Language
Place Intelligence Essentials provides the intelligence foundation that every local government organisation deserves — a comprehensive, current intelligence picture of your organisation and community, delivered through a professional intelligence platform designed for the way government works.

For organisations that have historically relied on incomplete internal data, manual research, or generic tools, Place Intelligence Essentials represents a significant step forward in the quality of evidence available for strategic decisions.

### Upgrade Path Language
As your intelligence needs deepen — particularly through strategic planning cycles, major capital investment decisions, or governance review processes — upgrade to Civic Intelligence Professional for expanded intelligence access, greater brief generation capacity, and dedicated Intelligence Partner support.

## TIER 2: CIVIC INTELLIGENCE PROFESSIONAL

### Tier Name
Civic Intelligence Professional

### Who It's For
Local government organisations of all sizes, management consultants and strategic advisors who regularly work with local government clients, regional planning bodies, and research professionals who require broader intelligence coverage and greater analytical depth.

Ideal for: councils with population 30,000–150,000 / consulting firms with regular local government engagements / research teams conducting systematic civic research / regional planning authorities.

### What Intelligence Access Is Included

**Civic Intelligence Centre**
- Full intelligence access for primary organisation and up to 3 secondary organisations
- Configurable intelligence focus for each team member's role
- Advanced Intelligence Signals with cross-organisation pattern alerts

**Intelligence Briefs**
- Unlimited Intelligence Brief generation across all 12 brief types
- Annual Comprehensive Council Intelligence Profile (deep-form, all dimensions)
- Quarterly Emerging Risk Intelligence Brief
- Annual Strategic Foresight Report
- Comparative Place Intelligence Assessments on demand

**Comparative Place Intelligence**
- Access to a customisable comparator group (up to 50 organisations)
- Full comparative intelligence access across all dimensions
- Sector-wide pattern analysis (all comparable organisations in selected context)

**Place Intelligence Database**
- Deep read access to intelligence for primary and secondary organisations
- Summary access across all 500+ LGAs in the database
- Document-level access for primary and secondary organisations
- Export capability (Intelligence Brief format and structured data)

**Team Access**
- Up to 20 named users
- Full role-based access configuration (Viewer, User, Administrator)
- Shared Intelligence Library
- Guest access for elected members and external advisors (up to 10 guest accounts)

**Support**
- Dedicated AIIPD Intelligence Partner
- Quarterly 60-minute Intelligence Review sessions
- Priority response for Intelligence Team queries (48-hour turnaround)
- Access to AIIPD intelligence webinars and professional development sessions

### Positioning Language
Civic Intelligence Professional is the platform tier where AIIPD's full intelligence capability becomes available. It provides the intelligence depth and breadth that strategic governance, professional consulting, and evidence-based policy work demands — with the team access, brief generation capacity, and partner support that organisations with serious intelligence needs require.

For local government organisations approaching a strategic planning cycle, undertaking a major community consultation, or making significant capital investment decisions, Civic Intelligence Professional provides the full suite of intelligence evidence that those processes deserve.

### Upgrade Path Language
For organisations managing intelligence across multiple jurisdictions, requiring custom intelligence products, or seeking a strategic intelligence partnership that extends beyond platform access, Civic Intelligence Enterprise provides expanded multi-organisation coverage, custom intelligence services, and the depth of analytical partnership that major government and consulting engagements require.

## TIER 3: CIVIC INTELLIGENCE ENTERPRISE

### Tier Name
Civic Intelligence Enterprise

### Who It's For
Large metropolitan local government organisations, state government agencies with local government intelligence responsibilities, major consulting firms with significant local government practices, and research institutions conducting major civic intelligence research programs.

Ideal for: metropolitan councils (population 100,000+) / state government agencies with LGA oversight / consulting firms with multiple active local government engagements / universities with civic research programs.

### What Intelligence Access Is Included

**Civic Intelligence Centre**
- Multi-organisation intelligence environment (up to 20 organisations as primary/secondary)
- Organisation-specific intelligence configuration for each primary organisation
- Regional intelligence synthesis capability
- Cross-organisation intelligence pattern analysis

**Intelligence Briefs**
- Unlimited Intelligence Brief generation across all 12 brief types for all covered organisations
- Custom intelligence brief types (developed with AIIPD Intelligence Team)
- Regional Intelligence Syntheses (up to 4 per year)
- Annual Intelligence Reviews for all primary organisations
- White-label brief formatting option (for consulting firms)

**Comparative Place Intelligence**
- Unlimited comparator access across the full Place Intelligence Database
- Custom comparator group design with AIIPD Intelligence Team support
- Regional and sector-wide comparative analysis
- Cross-state comparative intelligence

**Place Intelligence Database**
- Deep access across all covered organisations
- Full structured data export for research and analysis purposes
- API access for system integration (subject to integration agreement)
- Priority integration of organisation-specific documents

**Team Access**
- Unlimited named users
- Advanced role-based access configuration
- Single sign-on (SSO) integration
- Audit log and access reporting
- Unlimited guest access for governance contexts

**Support**
- Senior AIIPD Intelligence Partner (dedicated)
- Monthly strategic intelligence reviews
- Custom intelligence services (on-site briefings, workshop facilitation, custom research)
- Priority product support
- Early access to new platform capabilities

### Positioning Language
Civic Intelligence Enterprise is AIIPD's most capable subscription tier — the intelligence architecture for organisations whose decisions affect large communities, multiple jurisdictions, or the direction of the local government sector as a whole.

For state government agencies, it provides the regional and cross-jurisdictional intelligence capability that policy, oversight, and investment decisions require. For metropolitan councils, it provides the depth and breadth of intelligence appropriate to organisations managing billions in assets and services for large urban communities. For major consulting firms, it provides the intelligence infrastructure that their local government practice requires to deliver the highest quality evidence-based advice.

### Upgrade Path Language
Where specific intelligence needs exceed the standard Enterprise capability — particularly for multi-region analysis, national sector intelligence, major policy research programs, or strategic partnerships that extend the use of AIIPD intelligence — an Intelligence Partnership may be the appropriate arrangement. Contact the AIIPD Intelligence Team to discuss.

## TIER 4: INTELLIGENCE PARTNERSHIP

### Tier Name
Intelligence Partnership

### Who It's For
State and federal government agencies with significant local government intelligence responsibilities, national peak bodies, major research institutions conducting national-scale civic intelligence research, and organisations seeking a strategic intelligence partnership rather than a platform subscription.

Ideal for: ALGA (Australian Local Government Association), state departments of local government, national research centres, major infrastructure funding bodies, and organisations with custom or enterprise-scale intelligence requirements.

### What Intelligence Access Is Included

**Bespoke Intelligence Architecture**
All intelligence access is designed specifically for the partner organisation's requirements — there are no preset limits on organisational coverage, team access, brief generation, or database access. The Intelligence Partnership is structured through a negotiated intelligence services agreement.

**Full Platform Access**
- Complete access to all AIIPD platform capabilities
- All current and future feature releases
- Custom intelligence product development
- Dedicated platform configuration for partner requirements

**National and Regional Intelligence**
- National sector intelligence synthesis
- All-states regional intelligence access
- Cross-jurisdictional research capability
- Longitudinal dataset access for systematic research

**Custom Intelligence Services**
- Bespoke intelligence product design and production
- Policy intelligence synthesis (custom briefs for specific policy questions)
- Research program support (structured data provision, co-investigation arrangements)
- Executive intelligence briefings for ministerial or board-level contexts

**Technical Integration**
- Full API access
- Custom data integration arrangements
- White-label platform and intelligence products
- Integration with partner data systems

**Partnership Support**
- Executive Intelligence Advisor (AIIPD principal) as primary contact
- Quarterly strategic intelligence reviews at executive level
- Annual partner intelligence summit
- Co-authorship on published intelligence research (where agreed)

### Positioning Language
The Intelligence Partnership is AIIPD's highest-engagement relationship — designed for organisations whose intelligence needs and strategic objectives align with AIIPD's mission to improve the quality of evidence available for civic decision-making.

An Intelligence Partnership is not a software subscription. It is a strategic intelligence relationship — one where AIIPD's capabilities are deployed in service of a partner organisation's intelligence mission, with the depth of collaboration and customisation that only a partnership arrangement makes possible.

Intelligence Partnerships have included arrangements with state government agencies developing regional intelligence frameworks, peak bodies building sector-wide intelligence capability, and research institutions conducting landmark civic intelligence research. Each partnership is different, and each is structured around the specific intelligence mission of the partner.

### Upgrade Path Language
There is no tier beyond the Intelligence Partnership. The path forward from this tier is deepening the partnership — expanding coverage, developing new intelligence capabilities, co-creating intelligence resources that serve the broader civic intelligence ecosystem.

## PRICING NOTES (FOR INTERNAL AND PROPOSAL USE)

**Pricing philosophy:** AIIPD pricing reflects the genuine intelligence value delivered — not data volume or user count. The question is: what is the quality of decision-making worth to the organisation?

**Government procurement alignment:** Enterprise and Partnership tiers are structured for government procurement processes, with annual contracts, documented service specifications, and formal service level agreements. Procurement documentation is available on request.

**Research access program:** Academic institutions conducting systematic research may access the Place Intelligence Database through the Research Intelligence Access Program — a separately structured data access arrangement at pricing calibrated for research contexts.

**Community access program:** Registered not-for-profit community organisations may access Community Intelligence Profiles and Place Intelligence through the Community Access Program at a subsidised rate.

**Consultant project access:** Individual consulting project access to the Place Intelligence Database is available through a time-limited Project Intelligence Access arrangement — designed for consulting engagements that do not require ongoing platform access.
