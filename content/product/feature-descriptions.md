**Purpose:** Complete product feature descriptions for website, proposals, and sales materials. ## FEATURE 1: CIVIC INTELLIGENCE CENTRE

**Was:** Dashboard

### One-Line Description
The Civic Intelligence Centre is the primary workspace where civic intelligence for your organisation is synthesised, monitored, and explored.

### Three-Paragraph Executive Description

The Civic Intelligence Centre is the heart of the AIIPD platform — the intelligence workspace from which users access, generate, and navigate the full range of civic intelligence available for their organisation and place. Unlike a conventional dashboard that displays pre-set metrics and charts, the Civic Intelligence Centre is a dynamic intelligence environment that adapts to the user's role, priorities, and current intelligence needs.

At a glance, the Civic Intelligence Centre presents an Intelligence Summary — a synthesised, multi-dimensional snapshot of the current intelligence picture for your primary organisation. Governance intelligence, financial intelligence, community intelligence, infrastructure intelligence, and strategic intelligence are visible simultaneously — not as scores or rankings, but as analytical intelligence panels that communicate what the evidence means, not just what it shows.

The Civic Intelligence Centre is also where emerging intelligence signals surface — developing conditions, new patterns, and timely intelligence that warrants attention. Users can configure their signal preferences, explore the full Place Intelligence Database, generate Intelligence Briefs, and access their Intelligence Library — all within a single, coherently organised intelligence environment designed for professional use.

### Key Benefits
- Integrated intelligence view across all civic dimensions for your organisation
- Continuous intelligence updating as new source documents are integrated
- Role-adaptive configuration that surfaces the most relevant intelligence for each user
- Intelligence signals that alert users to emerging conditions without information overload
- Direct access to Intelligence Brief generation, comparative analysis, and the Place Intelligence Database

### Audience Fit
All users. Primary configuration is tailored for each user's role — CEO, Director, Elected Member, Strategic Planner, Consultant, or Researcher.

### Visual Treatment Suggestion
Clean, card-based layout with intelligence domain panels. Intelligence summary at top. Signal feed on right rail. Brief and analysis navigation on left. No gauges, no traffic lights, no scoreboards. Analytical narrative visible in each panel without clicking through. Premium, executive-appropriate design.

## FEATURE 2: PLACE INTELLIGENCE DATABASE

Australia's most comprehensive, curated, and continuously integrated repository of structured civic intelligence — spanning every LGA with deep longitudinal records.

The Place Intelligence Database is the foundational asset of AIIPD Civic Intelligence™ — the comprehensive, curated, and continuously integrated repository of structured civic intelligence from which all platform intelligence is derived. The Place Intelligence Database is what makes AIIPD genuinely different from any other civic technology platform. It is not a data warehouse — it is an intelligence repository, maintained through continuous curation, structural interpretation, and analytical enrichment. Access to the database through the AIIPD platform is access to years of systematic civic intelligence work — a research and analytical investment that no individual organisation could replicate independently.

- Quality assurance at every integration stage
- Accessible through the platform without requiring data science expertise

## FEATURE 3: COMPARATIVE PLACE INTELLIGENCE

**Was:** Council Comparison

### One-Line Description
Contextualised intelligence on how comparable organisations approach similar challenges — without rankings, focused on learning and strategic insight.

### Three-Paragraph Executive Description

Effective strategic decision-making requires context. Comparative Place Intelligence is AIIPD's capability for exploring how comparable organisations and places relate to your own — surfacing patterns, shared challenges, divergent approaches, and intelligence that is only visible when examining multiple jurisdictions simultaneously.

Comparative Place Intelligence is explicitly designed as a learning capability, not a ranking mechanism. Comparators are selected for genuine contextual similarity — geography, population, economic base, governance structure, financial scale — ensuring that comparisons are meaningful rather than misleading. Intelligence is presented as a relational landscape, not a league table. Users see where their organisation sits in a context of comparable organisations, understand what contextual factors explain differences, and identify approaches from comparable jurisdictions that are worth understanding.

The capability supports both targeted comparison (exploring a specific question in the context of comparable organisations) and pattern intelligence (identifying what is common, divergent, or unusual across a peer group). For state government and regional bodies, Comparative Place Intelligence scales to cross-jurisdictional and regional analysis — providing the multi-LGA intelligence that regional policy and planning requires.

### Key Benefits
- Meaningful comparison based on genuine contextual similarity
- Learning-oriented framing that builds openness rather than defensiveness
- Pattern identification across peer groups — not just pairwise comparison
- Customisable comparator group selection
- Narrative comparative intelligence alongside data visualisations
- Scale from pairwise comparison to full regional intelligence synthesis

### Audience Fit
Local Government CEOs and Executive Leadership, State Government Agencies, Regional Planning Bodies, Policy Makers, Management Consultants, Academic Researchers.

### Visual Treatment Suggestion
Relational landscape visualisation — selected organisation and comparators positioned contextually, not ranked. Side-by-side intelligence cards for selected dimensions. Pattern highlight callouts for shared and divergent findings. Comparator similarity score visible (to demonstrate why the comparators are meaningful).

## FEATURE 4: GOVERNANCE INTELLIGENCE REVIEW

**Was:** Compliance Check / Governance Performance Review

### One-Line Description
Deep analytical intelligence on governance structures, decision-making patterns, accountability mechanisms, and the conditions for effective, trusted governance.

### Three-Paragraph Executive Description

Governance is not just a compliance obligation — it is the architecture through which an organisation relates to its community, makes decisions, and fulfils its democratic responsibilities. The Governance Intelligence Review is AIIPD's analytical capability for understanding governance quality — the depth, effectiveness, and cultural dynamics of how an organisation governs itself.

Drawing on council meeting minutes, governance documentation, decision records, audit outcomes, accountability frameworks, and comparative governance intelligence from similar organisations, the Governance Intelligence Review produces a multi-dimensional governance picture. The Review is designed for governance improvement, not governance judgment. Its intelligence is framed to help leadership understand where governance quality is strong and where it can be developed — with reference to the experience of comparable organisations and the evidence of what effective governance looks like in similar contexts. It is the intelligence that CEOs, Mayors, and governance advisors need to lead governance improvement with confidence.

### Key Benefits
- Multi-dimensional governance picture beyond compliance assessment
- Evidence-grounded analysis of decision-making quality and patterns
- Comparative governance intelligence from similar organisations
- Improvement-oriented framing that builds openness rather than defensiveness
- Intelligence tailored for different governance audiences (CEO, Mayor, Council, Audit Committee)
- Designed for use in governance improvement processes and strategic governance reviews

### Audience Fit
CEO, Mayor and Elected Council, Governance and Legal Advisors, Audit and Risk Committee, State Government Oversight Bodies.

## FEATURE 11: DRMC FRAMEWORK

**Was:** Proprietary methodology (undescribed)

### One-Line Description
The Dynamic Relational Model of Consciousness — AIIPD's proprietary analytical framework that powers relational, multi-dimensional civic intelligence.

### Three-Paragraph Executive Description

The Dynamic Relational Model of Consciousness (DRMC) is the analytical framework that underpins all intelligence generated through AIIPD Civic Intelligence™. It is not a feature to be selected — it is the architectural foundation through which the platform understands and interprets civic complexity.

DRMC provides the analytical architecture for understanding places and organisations as relational systems — environments where governance structures, financial conditions, community dynamics, infrastructure realities, and environmental factors interact, reinforce, and sometimes contradict each other. Standard data analysis examines dimensions in isolation; DRMC analysis examines the relationships between dimensions. This relational orientation produces intelligence that is qualitatively different — revealing patterns, dynamics, and signals that single-dimension analysis systematically misses.

Practically, DRMC powers four distinctive intelligence capabilities: relational pattern recognition (identifying how dimensions interact), contextual interpretation (understanding what evidence means in specific contexts), emergent signal detection (identifying developing patterns before they crystallise), and multi-dimensional synthesis (producing intelligence that integrates all dimensions coherently). Every Intelligence Brief generated through AIIPD carries the analytical imprint of DRMC — whether the user is aware of it or not.

### Key Benefits
- Relational analysis that captures multi-dimensional civic complexity
- Contextual interpretation that honours the specificity of each place
- Emergent signal detection identifying developing patterns early
- Consistent analytical framework across all intelligence outputs
- Intellectual credibility grounded in a named, documented methodology
- Defensible methodology for government procurement and academic research contexts

### Audience Fit
Technical buyers, procurement decision-makers, academic researchers, and policy makers who want to understand the methodological foundations of AIIPD's intelligence quality.

### Visual Treatment Suggestion
Conceptual DRMC framework diagram showing the relational dimensions and how they interact. Restrained, academic visual style — not a marketing diagram but an intellectual framework illustration. Downloadable DRMC Framework Overview document for interested technical audiences.

## FEATURE 12: COLLABORATIVE INTELLIGENCE

**Was:** Team/sharing features

### One-Line Description
Team intelligence workspaces, intelligence sharing, and collaborative analysis — enabling organisations to build shared intelligence understanding across their leadership team.

### Three-Paragraph Executive Description

Intelligence is most powerful when it is shared. AIIPD's Collaborative Intelligence capability enables organisations to build a shared evidence culture — ensuring that the intelligence available through the platform informs the full leadership team, not just the individual user who generated a particular brief.

Team members can be invited to the organisation's intelligence environment at appropriate access levels — from Intelligence Viewers who can see all available intelligence, to Intelligence Users who can generate new briefs and analyses, to Intelligence Administrators who manage team configuration. Intelligence Briefs can be shared within the team, annotated with context notes, and organised in a shared Intelligence Library accessible to all relevant team members.

Collaborative Intelligence also supports governance contexts: Intelligence Briefs can be packaged as governance briefing sets for elected member distribution, shared with audit and risk committees, or made available to external advisors with appropriate access controls. The result is an organisation-wide intelligence environment — not a series of individual subscriptions, but a shared analytical resource that builds collective evidence capacity.

### Key Benefits
- Role-based team access across the full intelligence environment
- Shared Intelligence Library for organisational knowledge management
- Intelligence Brief sharing for governance and committee contexts
- Annotation and context-note capabilities
- External stakeholder access controls for advisory and oversight contexts
- Audit trail of intelligence access and generation activity

### Audience Fit
CEO and Executive Leadership Team (as the primary organisational intelligence administrators), all staff with intelligence roles, Governance and Legal Advisors, Audit and Risk Committee.

### Visual Treatment Suggestion
Team intelligence workspace view showing shared briefs, team member activity, and collaborative annotations. Clean, professional team management interface. Access level visualisation showing what each role can see and do.
