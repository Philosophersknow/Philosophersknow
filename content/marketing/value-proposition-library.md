## PART 7: FAQ RESPONSES

*Twenty Q&As addressing common questions across audiences. Intelligence framing throughout.*

**Q1: What is AIIPD Civic Intelligence™?**
AIIPD Civic Intelligence™ is an intelligence platform purpose-built for local government, policy, and place. It synthesises a wide range of civic data sources — annual reports, budgets, strategic plans, infrastructure records, demographic data, governance documents, community consultation outputs, and comparative data from across Australia — into structured, evidence-based intelligence for government, policy, and advisory professionals.

**Q2: How is AIIPD different from a benchmarking tool?**
A benchmarking tool measures and ranks. AIIPD generates intelligence — synthesis of evidence across multiple dimensions into contextualised, interpretive understanding that supports decisions. Where benchmarking produces a score, AIIPD produces insight. Where benchmarking asks "how do you compare?", AIIPD asks "what do you need to understand?" The two are fundamentally different in purpose, methodology, and value.

**Q3: What kind of data does AIIPD integrate?**
AIIPD integrates annual reports and financial statements, strategic and community plans, infrastructure and asset management plans, budget documents, council meeting minutes, community consultation reports, demographic and population data, environmental and spatial data, governance and accountability records, and comparative data from comparable Australian jurisdictions. The platform continuously integrates new sources as they are published.

**Q4: Who uses AIIPD?**
AIIPD is used by local government CEOs and executive leadership teams, Mayors and elected members, state government agencies, policy makers, management consultants, academic researchers, community organisations, infrastructure and planning professionals, and investors and funding bodies. The platform is designed for any professional whose consequential work requires reliable, structured civic intelligence.

**Q5: Is my organisation's information kept confidential?**
Yes. Intelligence generated specifically for your organisation is treated as organisational property and is not used for external purposes without explicit agreement. All data is stored in Australian data centres. AIIPD handles information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and applicable state privacy legislation.

**Q6: How current is the intelligence?**
AIIPD continuously integrates new source documents as they are published. The currency of intelligence for any specific dimension depends on the publication cycle of the underlying source data — annual reports, for instance, are integrated after publication and audit completion. The platform shows the last-updated date for each intelligence dimension.

**Q7: Can AIIPD intelligence be used in council reports and strategic plans?**
Yes. Intelligence generated through AIIPD can be cited and used in council reports, strategic plans, funding applications, policy submissions, and research publications, subject to the attribution requirements in your access agreement.

**Q8: Does AIIPD replace our internal reporting and planning processes?**
No. AIIPD is an intelligence resource that supports and enhances your existing processes — it does not replace them. It provides the evidence foundation and comparative context that makes your internal planning, reporting, and governance conversations more informed and more effective.

**Q9: How does the DRMC framework work?**
The Dynamic Relational Model of Consciousness (DRMC) is AIIPD's proprietary analytical framework. It analyses the relational patterns between different intelligence dimensions — understanding how governance, financial, community, infrastructure, and environmental factors interact and influence each other. This relational analysis produces intelligence that goes beyond what any single-dimension analysis can reveal. A DRMC Framework Overview is available on request.

**Q10: What intelligence products does AIIPD generate?**
AIIPD generates twelve core intelligence products: Council Intelligence Profile, Place Intelligence Report, Governance Intelligence Review, Community Intelligence Profile, Comparative Place Intelligence Assessment, Infrastructure Intelligence Brief, Financial Intelligence Review, Strategic Foresight Report, Emerging Risk Intelligence Brief, Regional Intelligence Synthesis, Executive Intelligence Summary, and Annual Intelligence Review. Custom intelligence products can be designed for specific organisational needs.

**Q11: How long does it take to get started?**
For local government organisations, onboarding typically takes four weeks from contract signature to first intelligence output. This includes an intelligence briefing, platform configuration, data integration of your organisation's specific documents, and team orientation. State government and research access arrangements have a similar timeline.

**Q12: Can I access intelligence for councils other than my own?**
Yes. Access to intelligence for other organisations depends on your subscription tier. The Place Intelligence Database contains intelligence for all Australian LGAs. Depending on your access level, you may have access to individual organisation profiles, regional intelligence sets, or the full national database.

**Q13: Is AIIPD appropriate for small rural councils?**
Yes. AIIPD is specifically designed to provide small and rural councils with the intelligence capabilities that were previously only available to larger, better-resourced organisations. The platform's intelligence synthesis capability is particularly valuable for organisations with limited internal analytical capacity.

**Q14: How does AIIPD handle data quality variation across councils?**
AIIPD's data integration process includes quality assessment and gap identification. Where source data is of limited quality, inconsistent, or missing, the platform flags the limitation in the relevant intelligence dimension and adjusts confidence indicators accordingly. Intelligence quality is transparent — users can always see what evidence underpins each intelligence output.

**Q15: Can AIIPD generate custom intelligence reports for specific topics?**
Yes. In addition to the standard intelligence product suite, AIIPD's Evidence Synthesis Engine can generate custom intelligence briefs for specific topics, decisions, or policy questions. Custom intelligence requests are handled by the AIIPD Intelligence Team as a service component of enterprise and government access tiers.

**Q16: What does an AIIPD subscription cost?**
Pricing depends on the access tier, the scope of intelligence coverage, and the volume of intelligence products required. AIIPD offers organisation subscriptions for local and state government, professional access subscriptions for consultants and advisors, research access arrangements for academic institutions, and community access for not-for-profit organisations. Contact the AIIPD Intelligence Team for a proposal tailored to your context.

**Q17: How is AIIPD different from government data portals?**
Government data portals provide access to raw data. AIIPD provides intelligence — synthesised, interpreted, and structured for decision-making use. The difference is the analytical layer that transforms data into understanding. AIIPD also integrates data from multiple sources — including non-government sources — into a coherent intelligence picture that no single data portal can provide.

**Q18: Is AIIPD built on AI?**
AIIPD uses AI capabilities as part of its Evidence Synthesis Engine — for document processing, pattern identification, and intelligence generation. However, AIIPD makes no exaggerated claims about AI capability. The platform's intelligence outputs are grounded in genuine evidence, produced through structured analytical frameworks, and reviewed for quality. AI is a tool in the intelligence generation process, not a replacement for rigorous analytical work.

**Q19: Can AIIPD support infrastructure grant applications?**
Yes. Infrastructure Intelligence Briefs generated through AIIPD provide the evidence base that infrastructure grant applications require — documenting asset condition, investment need, community demand, and financial sustainability context. AIIPD intelligence has been used to support grant applications to state and federal infrastructure funding programs.

**Q20: How do I get access to AIIPD for my organisation?**
Request a Platform Briefing through the AIIPD website — a 30-minute structured conversation with the AIIPD Intelligence Team about your organisation's context and intelligence requirements. Following the briefing, you will receive a tailored access proposal. Alternatively, contact the Intelligence Team directly at the contact details on the AIIPD website.
