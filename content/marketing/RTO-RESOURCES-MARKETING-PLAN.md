## 1. Executive Summary

AIIPD Civic Intelligence has developed a suite of RTO training package resources covering nine industry areas across Australia and internationally. These resources serve two distinct primary markets — **students** seeking assessment support and guided learning, and **RTOs** seeking compliance evidence, scope additions, and a pathway to CRICOS registration for international student delivery.

The resources are underpinned by the Dynamic Relational Model of Consciousness (DRMC) framework and delivered through a subscription platform featuring an AI Course Coach (Iris), a reflective journal (The Mirror), and assessment example banks — making this offer uniquely differentiated from generic training materials.

A third market — **CRICOS pathway support for eligible RTOs** — represents a high-value consultancy upsell once the primary resource sales establish market presence.

Target geographies: Australia (primary), United Kingdom (active secondary market), and broader international English-speaking VET/FE markets.

## 4. Market Segments

### Segment 1: VET Students (Australia)

**Who:** Current students enrolled with an RTO completing qualifications in CHC, BSB, CPP, CPC, SIT, or related packages.

**Problem:** Assessment tasks are complex, marking criteria are unclear, and students often lack access to a knowledgeable person who can answer specific questions about what is required. Many students are working adults studying in their own time with limited support hours.

**What they need:**
- Model assessment answers (what a good answer looks like)
- Step-by-step guidance through each unit of competency
- Clear, plain-language explanations of what evidence is required
- An on-demand guide who can answer "is this enough?" questions
- A way to track their own progress and reflective practice

**How we reach them:**
- Google search ("CHC43115 assessment help", "BSB assessment examples", "diploma community services assessment answers")
- Facebook groups for VET students (each training package has active student communities)
- TikTok and Instagram (short explainer content: "3 things your assessor is looking for in a community services assessment")
- Reddit: r/auslaw, r/australia, r/careerguidance
- Word of mouth via RTOs (referral partnership programme)
- Direct outreach via student Facebook groups

**Price sensitivity:** High — target AU$29/unit or AU$149/qualification. Free first unit as lead magnet.

**Conversion path:** Free sample → email list → $29 unit purchase → qualification bundle → subscription

---

### Segment 2: RTOs (Australian Registered Training Organisations)

**Who:** Registered Training Organisations seeking to expand scope, strengthen compliance evidence, or upgrade to CRICOS.

**Problem:** Developing compliant training and assessment materials from scratch is expensive, time-consuming, and requires specialist knowledge. RTOs applying to add qualifications to scope need evidence that they have the resources to deliver compliantly. ASQA audits increasingly focus on the quality and currency of assessment tools and learner support resources.

**Sub-segments:**
- **Small private RTOs** (1–20 staff): highest need, least capacity to develop materials internally
- **Mid-sized RTOs** (20–100 staff): want to accelerate scope additions without internal resource burden
- **TAFE and public providers** (100+ staff): lower priority for resource sales but potential for partnership
- **CRICOS-seeking RTOs**: have been operating domestically for 2+ years; want to enter international market

**What they need:**
- Training and assessment tools (TATs) mapped to current training package version
- Learner resources and trainer guides
- Mapping documents for RTO standards compliance (SRTOs 2015)
- Evidence of continuous improvement and learner support
- CRICOS application support: National Code compliance documentation, student support policy framework, ESOS obligations guide

**How we reach them:**
- LinkedIn (direct outreach to RTO managers, compliance managers, CEOs)
- Industry associations: ACPET, VELG Training, Australian Training Awards networks
- RTO consultant networks (they recommend resources to their RTO clients)
- Google search ("RTO training resources for sale", "CHC assessment tools", "SRTOs compliance materials")
- Training Package Assessing Organisations (TPA0) community forums
- ASQA "Preparing for audit" webinar participants (advertise in associated channels)

**Price sensitivity:** Medium — RTOs understand the cost of developing materials internally. AU$299–499 per qualification is reasonable; annual subscription at AU$245/month is competitive against custom development.

**Conversion path:** Free compliance checklist download → email nurture → compliance evidence pack purchase → subscription → CRICOS pathway consultation

### Segment 3: UK Further Education Providers

**Who:** FE colleges and independent training providers in England seeking AI in education briefings, governance frameworks, and training materials that map to UK qualifications.

**Problem:** UK FE providers face the same curriculum and assessment quality challenges as Australian RTOs, with the added pressure of AI governance requirements, Ofsted preparation, and Skills England qualification changes. They have fewer Australia-style resource providers to draw on.

**What they need:**
- AI in FE policy briefing (UK edition — already built)
- Staff AI literacy resources
- Assessment integrity policy templates
- Training resources for UK equivalents of CHC (Health & Social Care), BSB (Business Administration), CPP (Property), SIT (Hospitality & Catering), ICT
- Governance framework templates (Jisc-aligned, Ofsted-ready)

**How we reach them:**
- LinkedIn (target FE college SLTs, curriculum directors, quality managers)
- Association of Colleges networks
- FE Week, TES Further Education (advertising or contributed content)
- Jisc community networks
- Cold email to FE college principals and vice-principals

**Price sensitivity:** Medium-high — UK FE providers have CPD budgets. £75–£150 for reports; £39/month individual subscription.

**Conversion path:** Free AI in FE executive summary → full UK report purchase → subscription → training resource bundle for UK qualification equivalents

---

### Segment 4: International RTOs and CRICOS Pathway Market

**Who:** Australian RTOs who have been operating domestically for 2+ years (now meeting the post-5 December 2025 requirement) and want to apply for CRICOS registration to deliver to international students.

**Problem:** CRICOS registration is complex. It requires evidence of domestic delivery history, compliance with the National Code 2018, student support infrastructure, designated staff responsibilities, and specific documentation that many small-to-mid RTOs do not have readily available.

**What they need:**
- CRICOS readiness assessment (gap analysis against National Code)
- Student support policy framework (template + guidance)
- Marketing of courses to international students (guidance on ESOS obligations)
- Assistance with qualification CRICOS registration (per qualification)
- Ongoing compliance support post-registration

**How we reach them:**
- Direct LinkedIn outreach to RTO CEOs and compliance managers
- Post-audit follow-up (RTOs who have recently passed ASQA audit are ideal CRICOS candidates)
- ACPET and independent RTO consultant networks
- Google search ("CRICOS registration help", "apply for CRICOS Australia 2026")

**Price point:** AU$3,000–$8,000 per engagement (consultation + documentation package). High-value, low-volume.

**Conversion path:** Free CRICOS eligibility checklist download → email consultation → paid engagement

### Segment 5: Individual Practitioners and CPD Market

**Who:** Community services workers, property managers, business administrators, construction supervisors, and other VET-qualified practitioners seeking CPD resources or guidance on reflective practice for re-registration or professional development.

**Problem:** Many regulated professions require CPD hours. Practitioners need structured resources that help them demonstrate ongoing learning. The Mirror reflective journal + DRMC framework provide a structured, professionally credible approach to CPD evidence.

**What they need:**
- Reflective journal with structured prompts (The Mirror)
- Domain-mapped CPD evidence (demonstrates professional learning across specific competency areas)
- Short learning modules (not full qualifications)

**How we reach them:**
- Professional association channels (AASW for social work, REIQ for real estate, AIPM for project management)
- LinkedIn (professional development posts)
- Subscription as low-cost CPD solution (AU$39/month individual)

## 10. CRICOS Pathway — Detailed Offer

### Background

From 5 December 2025, applicants for CRICOS (ESOS) registration must demonstrate at least **two consecutive years of domestic VET delivery** before applying. This is a hardened requirement confirmed by ASQA and the Department of Education's 2025 fact sheet.

This creates a natural market: RTOs that have been operating since late 2023 or earlier are now eligible to apply. Many will not have the compliance documentation, student support infrastructure, or National Code 2018 alignment required.

### What We Offer

**Stage 1 — CRICOS Readiness Assessment (AU$1,500)**
- Review of current ASQA registration and scope
- Gap analysis against National Code 2018 (all 4 Parts)
- Student support framework assessment
- Written report: eligible / not eligible + specific gaps
- Delivered within 2 weeks

**Stage 2 — Documentation Pack (AU$3,500–$5,000)**
- Student support policy and procedure framework (National Code Part B)
- Marketing of courses to international students policy (National Code Part D)
- ESOS obligations guide for staff
- Critical incident register and student welfare procedures
- CRICOS application support documentation
- Course listing documentation for selected qualifications

**Stage 3 — Application Support and ISO Alignment (AU$2,000+)**
- Assistance completing ASQA ESOS application
- Liaison with state authority (where applicable)
- Preparation for ISO 21001 (Education Management Systems) accreditation
- Post-registration compliance calendar and obligations schedule

### Marketing Message for CRICOS Segment

> "You've done the hard part — two years of domestic delivery, an ASQA registration you can be proud of. The next step is CRICOS: the pathway to international students, higher revenue, and a genuinely global provider profile. We have the documentation, the expertise, and the accreditation roadmap. Let's get you there."
